This is an open access, open content and open source textbook in the form of a _[Mathematica](http://www.wolfram.com)_ notebook. If you do not have _Mathematica_ , you can open the notebook with Wolfram's free [CDF Player](http://www.wolfram.com/cdf-player/) software or view it in the Wolfram Cloud. You will not be able to do everything that you can with the notebook version, but it should be good enough for you to get an idea of what is included.
