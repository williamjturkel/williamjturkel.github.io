I'm a Professor of History at [The University of Western Ontario](https://history.uwo.ca/) in Canada. From 2018 to 2026 I was a member of the College of New Scholars, Artists and Scientists of the Royal Society of Canada. I'm currently revising a monograph on _Exploring Global 21st Century History with Generative AI_. I am also the author of _[Spark from the Deep](https://www.press.jhu.edu/books/title/10785/spark-deep)_ (JHU 2013) and _[The Archive of Place](https://www.ubcpress.ca/the-archive-of-place)_ (UBC 2007).
