# Tapera-DHH Survey of History Blogs

_October 10, 2006_  
Originally published at `https://digitalhistoryhacks.blogspot.com/2006/10/tapera-dhh-survey-of-history-blogs.html`

I've been working with Nicolás Quiroga of Tapera[^1] on a survey of history blogs. (To be fair, Nicolás has actually been doing most of the work.) Anyway, he has created a number of graphs of the preliminary results and posted them to his blog [1[^2], 2[^3], 3[^4]]. We also have a wiki page[^5] for the project on the Western Digital History server. The wiki is set up so that anyone can read it, but you need an account to edit it. I'm happy to provide access to the wiki to other digital historians who are interested in playing with or extending the results. If you'd like to participate in the ongoing blog survey, please mail your answers to the questions to Nicolás at tapera@tapera.info
