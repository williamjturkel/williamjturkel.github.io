# Digital History Interns 2006

_August 25, 2006_  
Originally published at `https://digitalhistoryhacks.blogspot.com/2006/08/digital-history-interns-2006.html`

The summer is over and the first five digital history interns at Western have completed the projects that they were working on: Jennifer Bonnell developed a place-based computing[^1] system for a field trip at the NiCHE[^2] summer school; Tim Compeau created the website An American Refugee: Joel Stone, United Empire Loyalist[^3]; Joel Ralph wrote a proof-of-principle demonstration for geocoding articles from The Beaver: Canada's History Magazine[^4]; Cat Rose worked on a historic driving tour of the Niagara region; and Rebecca Woods began a text mining project and helped to develop a graduate course in digital history[^5]. There is more information on the Digital History at Western internships page[^6]. I will be continuing the program next summer; please contact me if you are interested in it.
