# Frobisher: Search for Early Canadian History

_July 17, 2006_  
Originally published at `https://digitalhistoryhacks.blogspot.com/2006/07/frobisher-search-for-early-canadian.html`

My Digital History at Western[^1] server is now up, so I can host research tools. The first is an alpha version of Frobisher[^2], a prototype that mines information from the online *Dictionary of Canadian Biography[^3]* to refine Google[^4] searches. The user starts by entering the name of a person. If there are multiple possibilities (e.g., MacDonald), he or she is asked to clarify which person is of interest. Since the coverage of the *DCB* is limited to people who died before 1930, the system performs best with pre-20th-century queries. This is very much still research software: it does quite well on some things (e.g., distinguishing Sir Alexander Mackenzie[^5] from Alexander Mackenzie[^6]) and not so well on others (e.g., turning up useful material about William Connolly[^7]). There is more information about the design of Frobisher in a series of posts[^8] on this blog. I welcome any and all feedback at *wturkel@uwo.ca*.
